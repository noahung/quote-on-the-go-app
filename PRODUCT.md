## Design Context

### Users
Small businesses and freelancers in the UK managing client interactions, quotations, and invoices. They need a tool that is highly dependable, professional, but friendly and easy to use on the go.

### Brand Personality
Professional, dependable, friendly, and warm.

### Aesthetic Direction
Clean, minimal, and distraction-free. Utilizes Space Grotesk for headings and PT Sans for body text. Primary color is Orange (#f47421), background color is Light Gray (#F0F0F0), with Soft Lavender (#D8B4F8) accents. Focuses on flat design icons with subtle drop shadows.

### Design Principles
1. **Calm and Focus**: Subdue unnecessary visual noise and decoration, prioritizing white space and clean layouts so users can focus on high-priority alerts.
2. **Dependability**: Strong layout alignment and clear status indicators to build trust and confidence.
3. **Ruthless Simplification**: Progressive disclosure for secondary options, and keeping typography and styling unified.
# Confirmed reference direction (9 September 2026)

The user requested a simpler mobile home/dashboard and reusable components inspired by the Marshmallow insurance app screenshots, with a design specification that can later be used on the web. They explicitly chose to keep Quote on the Go's orange accent with warm cream surfaces. Apply the reference's clear hierarchy, bold friendly headings, generous spacing and simple rounded controls. Keep business tasks and accurate status information prominent.
